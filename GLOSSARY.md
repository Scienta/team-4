# Call for Papers

The conference's call for papers: speakers submit talks, reviewers rate them, the programme committee decides
which ones make the programme, and attendees register for the accepted workshops.

## People

**Speaker**:
A person with a registered profile (name, email, optional bio) who can submit talks. One email is one speaker,
whatever the letter case.
_Avoid_: Presenter, author, user

**Main speaker**:
The speaker who submitted a talk. Owns it: only they can withdraw it, and only their own talks count towards
the submission limit.
_Avoid_: Owner, submitter

**Co-speaker**:
A speaker added to someone else's talk to present it with the main speaker. A talk has at most two.
_Avoid_: Additional speaker, partner

**Reviewer**:
Someone who rates talks, known only by a number. Not a speaker.
_Avoid_: Rater, judge

**Programme committee**:
The group that decides on talks. Several members may decide on the same talk at the same moment.
_Avoid_: Committee member, admin, organiser

**Attendee**:
A person who registers for a workshop with a name and an email. Not a speaker and has no profile.
_Avoid_: Participant, visitor, guest

## Talks

**Talk**:
A session a speaker proposes for the programme. Has a title, a format, a level and a status.
_Avoid_: Submission (for the talk itself), session, proposal

**Submission**:
The act of a speaker proposing a talk. A speaker may have at most three talks submitted as main speaker.
_Avoid_: Application

**Format**:
The kind of talk, which fixes its length: workshop (120 min), presentation (45 min), lightning 20 or
lightning 10.
_Avoid_: Type, kind, duration (as a choice)

**Level**:
Who the talk is for: beginner, intermediate or advanced.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, audience

**Workshop**:
A talk in the workshop format. Once accepted, attendees can register for it.
_Avoid_: Course, tutorial

## Review

**Rating**:
A reviewer's score for one talk. A reviewer has at most one rating per talk; rating again replaces it.
_Avoid_: Vote (the 2019 predecessor), review, grade

**Score**:
The whole number from 1 to 5 in a rating.
_Avoid_: Stars, points

**Average rating**:
A talk's scores averaged, to one decimal, halves rounded up. A talk without ratings has none.
_Avoid_: Mean, rating (for the average)

## Acceptance

**Status**:
Where a talk stands: submitted, waitlisted, accepted, rejected or withdrawn. Every talk starts submitted.
_Avoid_: State, phase

**Decision**:
The programme committee accepting, rejecting or waitlisting a talk. Accepting needs ratings from at least two
different reviewers. Accepted and rejected are final.
_Avoid_: Verdict, review

**Waitlisted**:
Not accepted yet, but still in the running: the committee can still accept or reject it.
_Avoid_: Pending, on hold

**Withdrawal**:
The main speaker taking their talk out of the running, for good. Not possible once rejected.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, retraction

## Workshops

**Registration**:
An attendee's seat at one accepted workshop. One per email per workshop, whatever the letter case; registrations
stay if the workshop is withdrawn later.
_Avoid_: Signup, booking, ticket

**Capacity**:
How many registrations a workshop takes: 20, never more.
_Avoid_: Limit, seats (for the number)

## Programme

**Programme**:
Which accepted talk is on in which room and slot. A talk is on the programme at most once, and no speaker or
co-speaker is in two rooms in the same slot. Later changes to a talk leave the programme as it is.
_Avoid_: Schedule, agenda, timetable

**Room**:
One of the fixed rooms talks are held in: R1, R2 and R3.
_Avoid_: Track, hall, stage

**Slot**:
One of the fixed time periods of the day, the same in every room. One of them is long; a workshop can only go
in the long slot, other talks fit any slot.
_Avoid_: Time slot, session, period
